# IDEA LOSS & MISS ANALYSIS: WHY & WHERE DO IDEAS DIE?

---

## 1. THE 5 CORE CATEGORIES OF IDEAS YOU MISS

### Category 1: Work & Tech Solutions (System / Coding / Office Ideas)
* **When it hits:** System munnadi ukkandhirukum bodhu, code pannum bodho, or work problem pathi yosikum bodho.
* **The Idea Type:** "Indha task-ah automate pannidalam", "Indha bug-ku idhu thaan clean architecture", "Office process-ah simplify panna oru tool panna mudiyum".
* **Why it gets missed:** 
  * "Approm panni paathukalam" nu current meeting / current work-la continue aagiduvom.
  * Within 10 minutes, andha exact solution flash aana spark brain-la irundhu vanish aagidum.

### Category 2: Business & Startup / App Concepts (Product Ideas)
* **When it hits:** Bike ride, walking, shower, or bed-la thoonga poradhukku munnadi.
* **The Idea Type:** Pudhu website, AI tool, fitness app, or problem-solving micro-SaaS concept.
* **Why it gets missed:** 
  * Kai-la pen/paper illa or phone open panni notes type panna laziness.
  * "Naalaiku morning elundhu note pannikalam" nu ninaipeenga, morning elundha suthama blank aagirum.

### Category 3: Self-Upgrade & Skill Hacks (English & Learning Ideas)
* **When it hits:** Reel / YouTube paarkum bodho, illa yaarachu nalla speak/work pandratha paarkum bodho.
* **The Idea Type:** "Indha method-la English practice panna nalla varum", "Daily 15 mins indha book padikalam", "Indha concept padikanum".
* **Why it gets missed:** 
  * Next reel scroll panna odidum. 5 seconds of dopamine distraction kills the intention.

### Category 4: Social Commitments & Relationship Gestures (People Ideas)
* **When it hits:** Random-ah orutharoda nyabagam varum bodhu.
* **The Idea Type:** "Ivanukku help pannanum nu sonnome, call pannuvom", "Avan birthday-ku semmaya wish pannanum", "Family-ku idhu vangi tharanum".
* **Why it gets missed:** 
  * Immediate action eduka mudiyatha context (e.g. Work-la irukkom, travel-la irukkom).
  * Mental to-do list-la poduveenga, aana brain-ku buffer capacity illadha nala skip aagidum.

### Category 5: Personal Health & Body Fix Ideas (Fitness Spark)
* **When it hits:** Tummy thonguratha paarkum bodho, dress tight aagum bodho, or heavy tiredness varum bodho.
* **The Idea Type:** "Innaiku evening sugar illama black coffee mattum edupom", "Night 10:30 ku screen off panniduvom", "Tomorrow morning 6 AM walk poiduvom".
* **Why it gets missed:** 
  * Evening 4 PM craving or night 11 PM YouTube habit vandha udane, andha old biological pattern indha new idea-va overwhelm pannidum.

## 2. THE 4 ROOT CAUSES: WHY IDEAS DIE IN YOUR BRAIN

1. **The "Capture Friction" Trap (Takes > 5 Seconds):**
   * Phone unlock panni, Notes app thedi, open panni, keyboard la type pandradhukulla friction perusa irukku. 5 seconds mela aana brain drop pannidum.
2. **The "I Will Remember It" Illusion:**
   * Idea varum bodhu romba clear-ah thonum, so "idhava marakka porom" nu ninaipom. Aana human short-term memory half-life just 30 to 60 seconds thaan.
3. **The "Dopamine Washout" (Doomscrolling):**
   * Idea vandhadhum 1 reel or 1 notification paatha, brain pudhu visual dopamine rush-la munnaadi irundha thought-ah flush out pannidum.
4. **No "Next Action" Attached:**
   * Idea note pannaalum, adhai eppo execute pannanum (Next Action Step) nu assign pannala na, adhu dead note-ah maari poidum.
